# Storyboard: The Dungeon (31.5 s)

Music: "Sneaky Adventure", Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), first 31.5 s with a fade. 107 BPM, beats at 0.325 + n x 0.561 s.
Bar lines: 0.33, 2.57, 4.81, 7.05, 9.30, 11.54, 13.78, 16.02, 18.27, 20.51, 22.75, 25.0, 27.24, 29.48.

**Logline:** Wizard Clawd explores a dungeon: magics a stuck door open, falls through a trap door, gets swallowed by a gelatinous cube, and blows it apart from the inside.

**World:** one dungeon. Slate-blue stone, warm torchlight (orange glow on blue-grey), teal-violet magic. The cube is a translucent lime green with junk floating inside. Colour arc: warm torchlit corridor → a cold shaft → a green-lit lower room → a teal flash when the cube pops → a warm doorway at the end.

**Motif:** the magic door. It opens in A; in C Clawd opens a second one the same way and walks out through it.

**Clawd's arc:** determined → strained → idea → proud → cool → surprised → nervous → scared (fall) → dizzy → scared → determined → nervous (fizzle) → scared (inside) → idea → determined → disgusted (slimed) → proud.
