# Storyboard: The Butterfly (18 s)

Music: "Carefree", Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), first 18 s with a fade. 96 BPM, beats at 0.371 + n x 0.625 s.
Bar lines used for cuts: 1.0, 3.5, 6.0, 8.5, 11.0, 13.5, 16.0.

**Logline:** Clawd wants to catch a butterfly, but every grab misses and ends face-first in the grass, so Clawd gives up and sits, and the butterfly lands by itself on the flower Clawd crushed at the start.

**World:** one spring meadow. Soft sky blue, sap-green grass, cream/rose flowers; the butterfly is violet and rose with ochre spots so it pops on both sky and grass. Colour arc: cool morning (A) → bright midday run (B) → warm gold late afternoon (C).

**Motif:** the flower. The butterfly lands on a flower in A; Clawd's pounce squashes it and the flower ends up on Clawd's head (flower hat); in C the butterfly lands on that same flower, on Clawd.

**Clawd's arc:** happy → starstruck → mischief → dizzy → determined → ko → sad → surprised (small) → love.

**Shots:**

```

   reads: 0.0–0.6   iris opens: Clawd happy, swaying among flowers
          0.6–2.25  the butterfly flutters in from the top left and lands on the tall flower (Clawd's eyes follow it)
          2.25–2.9  Clawd: starstruck take
          2.9–4.0   mischief face, tiptoes toward the flower (side view)
          4.0–4.6   crouch + pounce; the butterfly lifts off at 4.3, just before Clawd lands
          4.6–5.3   Clawd lands in the flower: petals burst, the flower ends up on Clawd's head, dizzy
          5.3–5.75  butterfly hovers over Clawd's head, Clawd turns determined, looking up
          5.6–6.0   the butterfly zips off right; the camera whips after it

   reads: 6.0–7.1   running, butterfly just ahead
          7.1–7.9   jump 1 (7.25→7.87), arms up; the butterfly rises out of reach

          14.1–14.75 Clawd's eyes roll up, a small surprised take (small so the butterfly stays)

          16.0–17.95 [out: heart-shaped iris closes on the pair, holds, shuts]
```

Checklist: an event in every shot (landing/pounce, chase/dive, landing on Clawd). Reads run one at a time. Transitions at every seam: iris in, whip, brush wipe, heart iris out. No text. The ending rhymes with the opening (a butterfly landing on a flower, and this time the flower is on Clawd).
